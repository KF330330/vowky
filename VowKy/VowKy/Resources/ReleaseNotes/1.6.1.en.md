VowKy 1.6.1 Release Notes

- Fixed: the "Checking for updates…" window no longer lingers and overlaps the download window
- New update experience: download/extract/install progress now shares one window with the update prompt — minimizable to the Dock, cancellable during download
- Delta updates: upgrading from 1.6.0 downloads only a tiny patch; future updates will stay this small instead of re-downloading the full package
- Smaller download: the full installer shrinks from 228 MB to about 203 MB
