VowKy 1.4.1 Release Notes

- Fixed: the Settings window could get stuck behind all other windows and become unclickable
- Fixed: after closing the History or Transcription windows, app windows could no longer come to the front
- Fixed: a potential crash after repeatedly opening and closing the Settings window
- Improved: closing the onboarding window now counts as completing it — it no longer reappears on every launch
- New: "Reopen Onboarding" option in Settings → General
- Privacy: the local debug log no longer records recognized text, only character counts
