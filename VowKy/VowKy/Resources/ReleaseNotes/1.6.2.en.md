VowKy 1.6.2 Release Notes

- Link transcription now handles YouTube's sign-in check: when YouTube says "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", VowKy automatically retries with the browser cookies you chose in Settings; the JavaScript runtime yt-dlp needs is now bundled (existing users download about 38 MB extra on their first link)
- Faster, more reliable first-time tool setup: downloads come from the vowky.com mirror with real progress and speed, stalls or slow links automatically fall back to another source; channel and playlist links are rejected up front with a clear message
- Recording transcription gains an audio source picker: Microphone, System Audio, or Microphone + System Audio — pick the last one for online meetings to capture both sides (requires allowing VowKy under Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording)
- Fixed: recording could occasionally freeze the whole app until force-quit — microphone capture moved to a new backend, and VowKy now restarts itself automatically if the audio system stops responding
- Clearer failure messages: microphone / system-audio permissions, tool download errors and the log location are stated directly
