VowKy 1.6.0 Release Notes

- New "Recognition Speed" setting: switch between Standard speed (local engine, higher accuracy, now the default) and Fast mode (blazing-fast Apple engine)
- Much better speaker separation on long recordings: fixes hour-long recordings being split into too many speakers
- In Fast mode, dictation automatically falls back to the local engine for low-confidence sentences, reducing errors
- Various stability and polish fixes
