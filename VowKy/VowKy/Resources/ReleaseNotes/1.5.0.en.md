VowKy 1.5.0 Release Notes

- New: fully automatic "fast recognition engine" (macOS 26+) — much faster recognition that automatically switches between Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean based on what you speak, with mixed Chinese-English supported directly; mixed-language speech and Cantonese automatically use the local engine, and required language assets download in the background — zero setup, zero interruptions. (Note: right after switching to a new language, the first sentence may still be recognized in the previous language and catches up from the next one; older systems automatically use the local engine, unchanged.)
- New: speaker separation — recording transcription and file transcription can now distinguish speakers (Speaker 1, Speaker 2, …), with automatic speaker-count detection or a manual 2–6 setting.
- Faster recognition: the local engine now uses multi-threaded decoding, making long audio transcription noticeably faster.
- Fixed: longer recordings (roughly half a minute and up) could lose large parts of the final transcript.
- Fixed: the Settings window could get stuck behind all other windows; app windows could fail to come to the front after closing History/Transcription; a potential crash after repeatedly opening and closing Settings. Onboarding no longer reappears on every launch and can be reopened from Settings → General.
- Privacy: the local debug log no longer records recognized text, only character counts.
